Administrator Responsibilities
School Administrator permissions after activation:
  • Register and manage teachers.
  • Register and manage authorised school staff.
  • Assign teachers to classes.
  • Manage staff permissions.
  • Monitor attendance records and reports.
  • Manage school attendance settings.
The Super Admin retains control of the entire EDUNEXA platform.
