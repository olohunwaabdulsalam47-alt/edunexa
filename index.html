ATTENDANCE TECHNOLOGY

Flexible Ways to Verify Attendance

Schools can use suitable attendance methods according to their needs, devices and approved procedures.

✍️

Manual Teacher Entry

Teachers select attendance statuses for students in their assigned classes.

🔳

Student QR Identification

Scan school-issued student ID codes through an authorised attendance workflow.

📍

Campus GPS Check

Verify teacher location during attendance submission, with an authorised exception process when location is unavailable or inaccurate.

📷

Face Verification Integration

Potential support through compatible services, with appropriate consent, safeguards and alternative attendance methods.

👆

Compatible Fingerprint Devices

Possible integration with supported hardware. Availability depends on device and integration compatibility.

🔄

Automatic Synchronisation

Synchronise saved offline attendance when internet access returns, with a manual sync option as backup.

PRIVACY AND ACCOUNTABILITY

Designed Around Responsible Access

Attendance information should be available only to people authorised to see it.

🔐

Role-Based Access

Separate permissions for Super Admins, school leaders, teachers, students and guardians.

📋

Attendance Audit Trail

Keep records of submissions, correction requests, reviews and authorised changes.

🏫

School Data Separation

Each school should access only its own authorised records and linked users.

Biometric and location features require appropriate technical safeguards, consent where applicable, and alternative procedures. No method guarantees perfect identity or physical-presence verification.
