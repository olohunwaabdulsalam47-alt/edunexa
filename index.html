SIMPLE STRUCTURE

How EDUNEXA Is Intended to Work

A shared platform with separate, protected spaces for each registered institution.

1

Register Your Institution

An authorised representative submits the institution's information for registration.

2

Set Up Your School

Configure school type, academic structure, classes, departments and other settings.

3

Manage Your Institution

Authorised users access the modules and records assigned to their institution.

Every School Is Different. Your Platform Should Adapt.

Whether an institution uses terms or semesters, classes or departments, one curriculum or another, EDUNEXA is intended to support flexible configurations rather than force every school into one structure.
